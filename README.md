**Prioritization:** P1 primarily covers architectural design, planning and delivery at architect / architecte chargé de projet / chef de projet level; 5 years is the benchmark, not a rigid minimum. Roles requiring 8–15 years and chiefly interior/retail or chantier-only roles normally go into P2. A well-matched role elsewhere in France goes to P3. IT, cloud, data, electronics, enterprise architecture, and jobseeker self-advertisements are excluded entirely.

**Sort:** Each CSV and table is **posting date newest first**, then approximate distance from central Paris, then title. Exact dates are taken from the published listing; LinkedIn relative timestamps are **not converted to guessed absolute dates**, so undated records are last.

**Location/distance caveat:** For Paris city, CSV value 0 is a **city grouping indicator**, not a geocoded 0 km. Suburban distances are approximate town-centre proxies, and not a verified workplace-address 40 km check.

**Data-quality caveat:** **128 P1-labeled URLs do not equal 128 currently open, employer-unique, fully screened suitable jobs.** The first **107 P1 records were preserved unchanged** at the user's request. They have **not all been re-audited** under the updated 5-year seniority preference. URLs are deduplicated across the three files, but employer-identity duplicates hidden by agencies may remain. Listings older than a month may have closed. Check `verification_status`, `experience_requirement`, and `classification_reason` columns before applying.

**Application constraints worth noting:** The Hyphen Paris role requires existing Paris residency and data-centre experience; the HBS sports-event role requires professional English; the Vene Créteil hospital role requires prior healthcare-project experience. Application-form availability has not been confirmed for the whole list.
