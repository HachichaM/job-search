**Strict scope:** Building architecture only: architecte DE/HMONP/DPLG, architectural design and competitions, architecte chargé/chef de projet, architect assistant/collaborator, architect-led EXE/MOEX. Only France workplace locations in Paris or approximately within 40 km of central Paris; exclude software/cloud/data architects, jobseeker adverts, interior design-only and unlocated Île-de-France jobs.

**Sorting:** Original published date **newest first**; city-centre distance estimates break ties; then title. Published dates were checked against the **French-language individual pages** where possible; English language copies can show a different date format. Dates from reposts may not represent first publication.

**Distance:** All Paris-arrondissement records use the group proxy `0`, which means “Paris city,” **not a measured 0km distance**. Outside Paris, city-centre straight-line distances are approximate, not a verified radius based on the actual office address.

**Data quality:** Links are individual vacancy pages, but open application status remains unconfirmed. Old links may be expired. URL-level duplicates and some recognizable reposts were removed; confidential recruiters may still advertise the same employer vacancy. The figure **82** is a collected-link count, **not** a confirmed-open-jobs count.
