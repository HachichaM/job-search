### How to read the dataset

- **Job relevance:** P1 is primarily *architecte DE/HMONP, architecte confirmé, chef/chargé de projet, conception architecturale, concours, logements, équipements publics, patrimoine, réhabilitation*. Approximately five years' experience is the benchmark; some postings require slightly fewer or more. No software, IT, cloud or enterprise architect roles.
- **Geography:** P1 is Paris and locations provisionally within ~40 km from central Paris, P3 is elsewhere in France. Paris positions record `0` in the numeric distance field as a **Paris-city marker**, not a measured zero kilometre workplace distance. Suburb distances are estimates, not exact geocoded workplaces. Borderline locations require follow-up.
- **Sorting:** In each CSV and table, the **original displayed publication date is descending** (newest first), followed by Paris proximity, then role name. LinkedIn postings with relative-only age are recorded with a **blank publication date** and appear after all precisely dated postings.
- **Source:** The platform and direct individual job-page URL are recorded for every item, including LinkedIn; generic job-search links are not counted as listings.
- **Status:** The numeric totals represent **unique individual source URLs**, **NOT 174 confirmed, actively accepting, employer-unique vacancies**. Posting pages can stay online after a position closes. Explicitly old listings may be historical. Duplicates can exist under confidential employers/recruiters even with unique URLs. The `verification_status` field makes this uncertainty visible.
- **Legacy policy:** The initial 107 P1-labeled records were retained at the user's request, not retroactively reclassified. They have not all been independently audited against the ~5-year benchmark. The P1 total is therefore a **research pool**, not a fully qualified applicant shortlist.
