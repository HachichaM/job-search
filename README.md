## Scope and quality policy

Only **building architecture jobs** in France: architecte DE/HMONP/DPLG, architect-led project management and conception, architectural agency assistant, architect-led EXE/MOEX, and architecture stages. P2 jobs such as interior architecture, landscape, standalone BIM drafting and urbanism remain deferred. IT/cloud/data architect roles, jobseeker advertisements, foreign jobs and search-result pages are excluded.

**Sort precedence:** P1 first, **original published date newest to oldest**, approximate Paris proximity tiebreaker, then job title. The **undated listing** is placed last, not treated as recent. Publication timestamps may reflect reposts.

**Location:** Paris arrondissement is coded `0` as a **Paris-city grouping**, **not a measured zero-kilometre distance**. Suburban city-centre distances are estimates and exact workplaces are not yet geocoded. The 40 km radius is a working screen, not yet a per-address verified distance.

**Status:** These are 105 distinct source URLs. It is **not established that all 105 postings are open, independently unique employer vacancies or eligible for every applicant**. The CSV records verification status. Historical/older postings may have expired; employers may advertise the same confidential position through multiple recruiters.
