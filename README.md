### Selection rules

1. **P1 (top priority):** architecte DE/HMONP/DPLG, architecte confirmé, chargé/chef de projet architecture, independent building-design lead, concours, logement, ERP and rehabilitation *with meaningful design work*. Profile benchmark **around 5 years** (5–6 ideal); don't require 10 years. Job must be based in Paris or a nearby municipality plausibly within ~40 km.
2. **P2:** still useful, within the Paris working radius, but interior / retail architecture, predominantly chantier / DET / MOEX, project delivery without architecture-design ownership, early-career roles, or significant overqualification (8–15 years). **No IT architecture.**
3. **P3:** meaningful real-building architecture anywhere else in **France**, e.g., Bordeaux or Lyon. Location takes precedence: even a highly relevant role goes to P3 if outside the working Paris radius.
4. **Absolute exclusions:** IT/cloud/SI/data architects, candidate *emploi-demande* adverts rather than employers' vacancies, wrong countries, generic search pages, and links invented from guessed IDs.

### Reading and sorting

**Each tier is separately sorted by the publication date shown in the source, newest first**. When publication dates tie, proximity to central Paris then role title breaks ties. LinkedIn relative-time notices (e.g. “il y a 1 semaine”) without an exact publication date are **NOT** assigned an invented date: these rows are marked **Unknown** and sort last in their tier. Snapshot ages are calculated as of 2026-11-09.

**Distance warning:** Paris jobs store `0` as a *categorical Paris-city marker*, NOT literal 0 km. Suburb distances are approximate city-centre straight-line estimates, not door-to-door travel times. No per-workplace formal geocoding yet.

**Important legacy disclosure:** The original **107 P1-tagged records remain intact** as requested and **have not all been re-audited under the updated ~5-year P1 criteria**. This pass appended **13 newly screened P1 roles**; the **120** P1 figure is the **working candidate pool**, not 120 independently validated 5-year vacancies. Some earlier records include junior roles, internships, over-senior jobs or chantier-only specialisms and will need auditing later, without silently deleting them.

**Open status:** An individual URL was located, not every vacancy confirmed currently accepting applications. Older posts may be expired. Some recruiters may represent one vacancy across more than one platform. Matching by URL removes direct link duplicates, but anonymized employer duplicates need human review. Unverified links do not become “open” merely because they are public.
