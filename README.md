## Qualification, ordering, data-quality rules

- **P1 primary intent:** Physical building architecture, conception, architectural study/management, concours, réhabilitation, logement, hospital/ERP and project coordination. Aim for **about 5 years of professional experience**, not an assumed 10 years. A role asking for 3–6 years can still be relevant; 7–15-year requirements and predominantly chantier-only work normally belong in P2.
- **Location:** P1/P2: workplace in France, Paris or plausibly within 40 km of its center. The CSV stores `0` for jobs in the municipality of Paris as a **category marker**, not a geocoded zero-distance measurement; suburbs show approximate municipality-level figures. Formal 40 km workplace-by-workplace verification remains pending. P3 means elsewhere in France.
- **Date first:** Every tier is sorted **descending by posting date**. Equal dates are secondarily ranked by approximate Paris proximity and then alphabetically by role. An ad whose exact posting date is unavailable appears **last**, not falsely labeled recent. LinkedIn relative timestamps are not assumed to be absolute publication dates.
- **Source links:** Only individual job advertisements or a direct employer recruitment page are kept. Search-category counts never count toward progress. Ads from the **Ordre des architectes** must be employer `emploi-offre`, not jobseeker `emploi-demande`; no IT/cloud/software architects.
- **Duplicate control:** Exact URLs are deduplicated across all three CSVs; confidential recruiter mirrors may still represent the same vacancy even with distinct URLs. **Some original P1 rows were deliberately retained without retrospective reclassification**, per user request; the label is therefore a **working pool**, not a certified profile match.
- **Verification:** An accessible or indexed job page does **not** establish that applications are still accepted. Historical links may be closed. Important conditions, such as the AREP 5-year requirement, healthcare-domain experience, bilingual English or Paris residency, can constrain eligibility. The CSV's `verification_status`, `classification_reason`, and `posting_date_confidence` provide the screening notes.
- **Special source issue:** A P3 Gouronnec Allaire listing says **July 2027** in its heading but **July 2026** in its body; the contract's end date needs employer confirmation before application. Do not infer a current opening from the discrepancy.
