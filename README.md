**Latest checked:** 2026-11-09 · **Target:** 500+ distinct P1 job postings · **Collected so far:** **48** direct job pages.

[**Download the full P1 CSV**](data/architecture_p1_paris_40km.csv)

> **Scope:** Building architecture jobs in France within a working radius of about 40 km from Paris. P1 = architecte DE / HMONP / DPLG, assistant or collaborateur architecte, architecte chef de projet, architectural conception, agency-based architectural construction/suivi and closely related project roles. **Not included:** IT/cloud/data architecture, electronics architects, job-seeker adverts, non-French postings or unverified search-result counts.

## Priority P1 — newest advertisements first

**Ordering:** P1 first; publication date descending (newest first); approximate distance ascending as a secondary key for equal dates; title alphabetically as a tertiary key. This is the **original displayed publication date**, not the date the ad was last crawled or updated.

**Distance disclosure:** Paris districts are grouped as `Paris commune` and stored as a distance proxy of **0 km** — **not a measured door-to-door or postcode distance**. Boulogne-Billancourt (~8 km) and Colombes (~10 km) are approximate city-centre straight-line distances. For exact distance ranking, the agency's complete street address and geocoding are needed. No outside-radius places are in this seed. Listings identified only as '77 on RER A' are excluded until their precise municipality is known.

## Dataset structure

The CSV is the primary source of truth, including `priority`, `posting_date`, `days_since_posted`, `location`, `distance_from_paris_km_approx`, `job_title`, `employer_or_recruiter`, `contract`, `specialty`, `salary_as_advertised`, `source_platform`, `direct_job_link`, `checked_on`, and `verification_status`.

**Verification status:** `job_page_accessible_application_not_confirmed` means an individual job page was found and inspected in public search, but **does not guarantee** that the employer is still accepting applications. A search-index page count never counts as collected. Publication dates are those exposed by the respective listing site or its index. Application URLs may lead to recruiter/login flow.

**De-duplication:** Do not count the same vacancy multiple times across HelloWork, ArchiBat, Jobalys, France Travail and the Ordre des architectes. A few postings may still represent the same underlying confidential-client vacancy; these require manual confirmation before reporting a final unique-vacancy total. This seed uses one preferred URL for obvious duplicates such as H.B. Architectes.
