## How listings are screened and sorted

- **Priority P1:** Approximately 5 years' experience is the benchmark (not 10). Preference for architecture of real buildings and meaningful responsibility for conception or design-led project delivery. Adjacent, over-senior and principally chantier/interior/BIM drafting positions normally go to P2. Geographic outliers go to P3, not discarded.
- **France only.** P1 workplaces are within Paris or provisionally near central Paris inside ~40 km. **Paris `0` is a city grouping proxy, not literally a measured 0 km.** Other reported distances are approximations to town centres; exact geocoded workplaces and strict 40 km screening remain incomplete.
- **Publication-date ordering:** Within each priority, date **newest to oldest**; same-day listings order by approximate Paris distance and then job title. **Unknown dates sort last**. Relative LinkedIn ages do not become invented exact dates.
- **One URL per source record:** Direct individual vacancy pages, recruiter job pages and a limited number of identifiable hiring posts; no generic job-search-result counts, no IT/cloud/software/data architect roles, and no job-seeker adverts.
- **Data integrity:** 212 distinct recorded URLs does **not** equal 212 independently confirmed currently open employer-unique vacancies. Some old vacancies may be closed; mirrored recruiters might advertise the same position behind different URLs. Published application forms and stated expiration dates still require validation. See each CSV's `verification_status` and `posting_date_confidence`.
- **Legacy P1 preservation:** The first 107 records were retained as requested, rather than retrospectively reclassified. Their strict fit to the new five-year profile has **not** all been independently audited. The 143 P1 count is a working source-link pool, not a 143-job verified shortlist.
- **Date-age units:** `days_since_posted` is a snapshot as of 9 November 2026. For continued collection, update both the checked date and age rather than silently using stale values.
