## Scope and ordering

- **P1:** Architecture du bâtiment; architecte DE, HMONP, DPLG; assistants and chef de projet architecture; conception, concours, logement, bureaux, patrimoine, MOEX explicitly asking for an architect. Selected adjacent roles are accepted when the architecture qualification and work are explicit.
- **Radius:** France only; workplace in Paris or a municipality approximately within 40 km from central Paris. Paris arrondissements use **0 as a grouping proxy**, not a literal measured distance. Other city-centre distances are approximate and still need precise geocoding.
- **Order:** Publication date **newest → oldest**, then proximity on same date, then job title. Reposts are not counted as new vacancies where a specific matching prior record is evident.
- **Dates:** Prefer date printed on the **individual job page**, which can differ from a listing directory's update/republication date. Where discrepancies were discovered, individual dates were corrected.
- **Status:** A direct individual job URL is identified, not necessarily a currently open application. Old postings may be expired. Duplicate confidential clients and current application availability still require further checks. **81 is not the number of independently confirmed open applications.**
