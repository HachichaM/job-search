[**CSV download / source data**](data/architecture_p1_paris_40km.csv)

**Definition:** P1 means a building-architecture job or a role explicitly recruiting an architect, including DE/HMONP/DPLG, chef de projet architectural, assistant architecte, chantier/MOEX, and architecte-projeteur architectural synthesis. IT/cloud/data architects and interior-design-only jobs are excluded. All workplaces are Paris or plausible municipalities within 40km of central Paris, France.

**Ordering:** Posting date descending; among listings on the same date, approximate proximity; then title. Date on a source listing may represent a new recruitment post or repost; do not interpret it as a guaranteed unique vacancy. The French-language employer/recruiter page is prioritized over internationalized mirrors when dates conflict.

**Distance:** `0 km` for Paris is a **categorical Paris-city proxy**, not a physical measurement. Distance for non-Paris municipalities is an approximate city-centre straight-line estimate. Exact address geocoding remains pending.

**Verification:** The 84 rows are **individual public job URLs**, not 84 verified open vacancies. Old records may have expired; recruiter duplicates may remain. Where source documents contradict a contract field, the uncertainty is explicitly marked in the verification status.
